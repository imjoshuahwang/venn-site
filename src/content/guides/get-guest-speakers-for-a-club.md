To get guest speakers for your club, choose a topic your members care about, email specific people with a short invitation and a clear date, and offer a virtual option. Most speakers say yes to a small, well-organized request that costs them 30 minutes.

## Step 1: Pick a topic and a date

"Careers in biotech" or "how I built my first product." Set a date, time and length, usually 30 to 45 minutes with questions.

## Step 2: Find speakers

College students and club leaders, alumni from your school, local professionals, parents' contacts, founders and teachers' contacts. Start with people who are one or two steps ahead of your members.

## Step 3: Write the invitation

Who you are, how many members, the topic, the date, the format and why you picked them. Say it is virtual or in person and how long it takes. Keep it under 100 words.

## Step 4: Make it easy

Offer a video call, send a calendar invite and share questions ahead of time. Offer to handle the tech and the introduction.

## Step 5: Prepare your members

Share the speaker's bio, collect questions and assign someone to host and keep time.

## Step 6: Follow up

Send a thank-you the same day with a photo or a note about what members learned. Ask whether they would recommend someone else.

## Step 7: Keep a speaker list

Record names, topics and contacts. Every event makes the next one easier.

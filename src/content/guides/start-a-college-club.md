To start a college club, check your school's registration rules, gather a few founding members and an advisor, write a short constitution and register with student activities. Then hold one event fast, because a club is real once people show up.

## Step 1: Check what already exists

Search your school's club directory. If a similar club exists, join it or find a different angle. Admins often reject clubs that duplicate another.

## Step 2: Learn the registration process

Ask student activities for the requirements: minimum members, advisor, constitution, officer roles and deadlines. Many schools also tie funding to registration.

## Step 3: Gather founding members

You usually need three to ten people. Ask friends, classmates and people in related classes. Choose a president, treasurer and secretary or the roles your school requires.

## Step 4: Find an advisor

A professor or staff member who cares about your topic. Bring a one-page plan and be clear on how little time you need from them.

## Step 5: Write a short constitution

Purpose, membership, officers, meetings and how decisions are made. Use your school's template if there is one.

## Step 6: Register and ask about funding

Submit the forms and ask what money, rooms and promotion registered clubs can access.

## Step 7: Run an event in the first month

A speaker, workshop, social or build night. Collect sign-ups and set the next meeting before people leave.

## Step 8: Plan for turnover

Document passwords, contacts and how-tos, and train future officers early.

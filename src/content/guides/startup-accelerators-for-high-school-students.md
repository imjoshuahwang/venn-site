To find a startup accelerator as a high school student, search for programs that accept your age, check cost and time commitment, and apply with something you have already built. Not every accelerator takes minors, so eligibility is the first filter.

## Step 1: Know what an accelerator does

It is a short program, often a few weeks to months, that gives mentors, structure and a deadline for your project, usually ending with a demo. Some give money and some take ownership. Read the terms.

## Step 2: Search for programs that accept your age

Look for youth, student and teen programs, plus college programs that allow high schoolers. Check the rules on age, location and parent consent.

## Step 3: Check cost and terms

Ask: is it free, what does it cost, does it take equity, who owns your work, how many hours a week. Be careful with programs that charge a lot and promise funding or admissions outcomes.

## Step 4: Look at proof

See what past participants built and what the program says about outcomes. If it cannot show real examples, be cautious.

## Step 5: Prepare your application

A clear one-liner, a demo or screenshots, any users or members, and what you plan to do with the time. Ask a mentor to read it first.

## Step 6: Get parent or guardian input

Contracts, equity and money need an adult. Share the terms before you accept.

## Step 7: Have a plan B

If you are not accepted, set your own milestones, find mentors and publish your progress.

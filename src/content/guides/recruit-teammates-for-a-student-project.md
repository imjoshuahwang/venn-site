To recruit teammates for a student project, define the roles you need, ask specific people with those skills directly and give them a small first task. People join projects that have a clear goal, a deadline and a job just for them.

## Step 1: Write the goal

One sentence on what you are making and when it is done. People commit to finish lines, not to ideas.

## Step 2: List the roles

Design, code, writing, research, outreach, video, organizing. Note how many hours each takes per week. Be honest about what you cannot do yourself.

## Step 3: Make a short list of people

Classmates, club members, friends of friends and people from online communities. Pick people for skills and reliability, not only friendship.

## Step 4: Ask directly

"Would you want to build the app's design? It is about 3 hours a week until March." A specific ask gets more yeses than a general call.

## Step 5: Start with a trial

Give each person one small task with a deadline. See who finishes. This is easier than removing someone later.

## Step 6: Set simple rules

Where you talk, when you meet, who decides, how credit is shared and what happens if someone leaves. Write it in a short doc.

## Step 7: Keep momentum

Weekly check-ins, visible progress and thanks. Teams drift when no one knows what is next.

To validate a startup idea as a student, talk to about 20 people who have the problem, learn whether they already pay or work around it, and test a simple version before you build the real thing. Validation means evidence that someone wants this, not that your friends think it is cool.

## Step 1: Write the idea as a problem

"People waste time on X because Y." If you cannot say who has the problem and how often, the idea is not ready.

## Step 2: Find 20 people to talk to

Students, clubs, teachers, local businesses or whoever has the problem. Avoid only asking close friends, who will be polite.

## Step 3: Ask about the past, not the future

Ask what they did the last time this happened, what they tried and what it cost. Do not ask "would you use this?" because people say yes to be nice.

## Step 4: Look for signs of real demand

Good signs: they have already tried to fix it, they spend time or money on it, they ask when it will be ready. Bad signs: polite interest and no action.

## Step 5: Run a cheap test

A landing page with a sign-up, a manual version where you do the work by hand, or a small pilot with one club. Set a number before you start, such as "10 sign-ups from 50 visitors."

## Step 6: Check competitors

Search for existing solutions. Competitors are often a good sign. Find out what people dislike about them.

## Step 7: Decide

Build, change the idea or stop. Stopping early is a win. Write down what you learned.
